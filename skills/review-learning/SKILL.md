# Review Learning

Resolve plugin resources and workspace paths using [runtime locations](../../docs/plugin.md#runtime-locations) before running commands. Keep personal data in the working workspace, never in the installed plugin.

Load complete selected-course history through inspect-state, retained objective contracts, effective calendar versions, explicit current UTC time and positive integer-second estimates. Call compute-schedule with review-v1; do not hand-calculate authoritative dates or use filesystem order.

The accepted policy uses 1–3–7–14 calendar-day intervals, then repeated 14. A qualifying independent review advances at most once per objective/session. Resolved failure or assistance schedules the next study day and retains stage. Unknown assistance, disputes and unassessable outcomes do not advance. Same-session lapse keeps the short follow-up even if followed by immediate success. Consult the [review contract](../../docs/learning-contracts.md#review-policy-and-event-ordering) for calendar revision rules.

Use the default 25% review allowance or scoped override. The calculation floors it to seconds, orders by original due date then objective ID, lets smaller due items fit, and shows oversized work deferred. Offer a meaningful split or explicit budget change; never silently lengthen the session. Unused allowance is available for new material. Missing days preserve original due dates and evidence.

Offer a manageable return after absence without demanding an explanation or inferring traits. Select a check suitable for the objective; a memorized answer does not establish transfer. Save answers, assistance and judgments using run-session and assess-answer. Record calculation provenance, policy/settings and time when publishing schedules. Rebuild after corrections or calendar changes; retain historical timestamps and due-date provenance.
